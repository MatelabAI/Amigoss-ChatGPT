THE BENEFITS OF BELONGING

More to share.
More to be part of.

From a coffee in Glebe to a night at the theatre, membership brings you closer to the community.

  • Stay in the loop

    Receive regular emails or letters with our agenda and events calendar.

  • Enjoy member discounts

    Get special discounts at our functions, workshops and events.

    Join the committee and get involved in planning and organising activities.

  • Connect across generations

    Be part of an intergenerational community of Spanish speakers and friends.
